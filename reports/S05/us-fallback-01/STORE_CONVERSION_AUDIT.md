# Store conversion audit — creative judgment, no runtime change

## Verdict

**Current icon: retain for v0.1.0; consider a later distinctiveness redesign. Current screenshots: technically useful product evidence, but need conversion framing/localization before a strong US store launch.** No download-rate experiment was run; suggestions are design hypotheses, not measured conversion gains.

The first-time visitor needs to understand a specific problem: lecture/PPT photos are unlikely to be revisited, yet “might be useful” makes deletion uncomfortable. The mechanism is two usable outputs: **PDF for a person to read; ZIP for an AI to work with**. The app itself is not an AI assistant. App onboarding remains teaching, with no promotional hero added.

## Name, subtitle and positioning

- **Lecture Asset:** specific to lectures but “Asset” is technical and vague to ordinary students. Retain the established name for this launch preparation; use the subtitle to make the outcome plain. A rename without search/brand testing is not the minimum useful change.
- **讲座照片整理:** immediately understandable utility name, less memorable as a brand. Retain for consistency; the first frame carries the emotional context.
- **Lecture photos to PDF and ZIP:** clear and accurate. “AI lecture summaries,” “lossless backup,” “clean your phone instantly” or “recover gigabytes” would overclaim current behavior.
- English listing already starts from reluctance to lose potentially useful slides and explains output/recovery/deletion limitations. The mechanism appears above the feature list. Keep its truthful capability/limits, shorten only the top visual hierarchy in a later conversion edit.

## Minimum redesign with highest practical value

1. **Rework first two store frames, not the app UI.** Frame 1 states the pain and shows actual ready UI plus a real synthetic PDF preview: CN “不常看，也舍不得删。” / EN “Rarely revisited. Hard to delete.” Frame 2 explains the outputs: CN “PDF 留着读，ZIP 交给 AI。” / EN “Read the PDF. Work with the ZIP.” Include actual app usage, not a text-only poster. New PDF preview asset is still missing.
2. **Use 01/02 as workflow proof, with one caption each.** “滑动选择，按拍摄时间排好” / “Select a batch. Keep capture order.” Do not imply dragging changes frozen chronological order.
3. **End on optional, safe cleanup or clearly labeled external AI use.** “先保存，再决定是否清理” / “Save first. Choose what to clear.” Show that keeping Photos is a visible option. Do not advertise deletion of Live Photo motion as preservation of the full original.

This can preserve three native screens (01, 02, 04), replace the weak 03 frame with actual PDF viewing, and either annotate 05 as illustrative or replace it with the explicit optional-cleanup state. Five frames are sufficient; extra slides are not the priority. Avoid screenshots with unfamiliar full-resolution technical metrics.

Apple permits text/image overlays around actual in-use screenshots; captions must remain accurate and age-appropriate. No review approval is implied by “permitted framing.” Never manufacture an in-app AI conversation or confidential lecture material. [App Review Guidelines 2.3.3, 2.3.7–2.3.9](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).

Share the icon, truthful app geometry, synthetic slide illustrations, PDF/ZIP glyphs, page-order motif and safety sequence. Localize UI captures and short captions, not just a Chinese screenshot's outside heading. CN can use “讲座/PPT”; US use “lecture slides.” Keep Chinese/English OCR capability accurate in both. Do not introduce WeChat integration, an AI provider logo or a cloud-upload claim. Store CTAs/badges must match verified regional availability; neither region is public today.

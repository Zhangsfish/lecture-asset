## Why this order

Scene → light selection work → two concrete outputs → human review → further AI
work → user-controlled cleanup. PDF and ZIP are distinct. AI is not introduced
until after the outputs exist. The final scene reassures without claiming automatic
safe storage verification or instant space recovery.

## Frame 5 wording and boundary

Final owner-directed copy: **Share the AI ZIP. Keep exploring the lecture.**
The short headline preserves ongoing learning beyond one summary; the smaller
boundary/qualifier identifies the external AI tool. The previous “Hand … to AI” phrasing is superseded.

“After export · AI tool example” uses the shared 42 px gray-blue subtitle style immediately above
the external workspace, which never sits inside the phone. “Example” denotes illustrative actions:
Summarize / Ask questions / Study notes / Lecture context. The image also says to
“Use an AI tool that can read images.” This small qualifier is secondary to the
action cards and headline. There is no model/provider logo,
real conversation, claimed generated answer, or universal compatibility promise.

## Frame 6 safety

“Confirm ZIP saved” refers to the user's explicit confirmation, not an App-verified
delivery receipt. Both cleanup choices are illustrative, outside the phone.
The capture shows the actual App source-delete confirmation after test-host-only
synthetic receipt staging. No real external save or deletion is claimed; Cancel is
tapped, and Photos/job retention is verified. The system PhotoKit deletion dialog
is deliberately not entered.
The image says “Source deletion needs separate confirmation.” The separate gates
remain valid ZIP/integrity, exact ZIP identity, reported ZIP share completion,
explicit external-save confirmation, fresh authorization/exact-source checks,
fresh user delete action and PhotoKit's confirmation/success. Keeping Photos and
clearing App files is a distinct, twice-confirmed action, not a deletion fallback.
No screenshot capture performs sharing, confirms external saving or deletes.
